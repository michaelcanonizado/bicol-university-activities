The code uploads two images and converts them into RGB format so their pixel values can be analyzed. It then converts the images into NumPy arrays and checks that both images have the same dimensions. Five random pixel locations are selected, and the RGB values at those exact same locations are extracted from both images. This allows us to compare how different lighting conditions affect the numerical RGB values of the same areas.

I then took a picture of an object with 2 different lightings: orange warm light and white cool light, whilst controlling other factors such as camera used, camera position, etc. When I inspected the same location for the two images and compared their RRGB values, i found that the pixel had greatly different values due to the different lighting conditions, with some values having a difference of 100-150 meaning that its greatly different
